Startup overview

Business model

ChainLedger AI is presented as a concept for helping small businesses organize energy-use data and estimate operational emissions.

↗

Problem

Smaller organizations may lack a simple way to collect energy data, estimate emissions and assemble a first-draft sustainability summary.

Proposed solution

A simple workflow for recording utility consumption, calculating indicative emissions, viewing trends and exporting audit data.

Potential customers

Small manufacturers, workshops, schools and businesses that need an initial view of energy use.

Possible revenue model

A future subscription for multi-user workspaces, reports and managed audits. Pricing shown in this prototype is illustrative only and no payments are processed.
